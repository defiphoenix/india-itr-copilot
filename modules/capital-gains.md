# Capital gains (Schedule CG / 112A / VDA)

## Bucket every disposal first — the bucket decides everything

| Asset sold | Long-term after | ST treatment | LT treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listed equity / equity MF (STT paid) | 12 months | s.111A special rate | s.112A special rate, first slice exempt |
| Non-equity ETFs (silver/gold/international), listed bonds | 12 months | slab | s.112 special rate |
| Debt MF bought on/after 1-Apr-2023 | never LT | slab always | slab always |
| Real property, physical gold | 24 months | slab | s.112 special rate |
| Crypto/NFT (VDA) | n/a | flat special rate, no basic-exemption adjustment, no loss relief | same |

Rates and exempt slices come from the rules registry, never from memory. Date-of-transfer matters
in years where a Finance Act changed rates mid-year — check the registry notes.

## Computation discipline

- Consideration − (cost of acquisition + improvement + transfer expenses). STT is never a CG
  expense; brokerage/exchange/stamp on the sale are.
- **Grandfathering** for pre-cutoff equity: cost steps up to the cutoff-date fair value (registry
  holds the date). The return wants scripwise rows for 112A — keep ISIN, dates, quantities.
- Buyback proceeds: check the year's regime — recent Finance Acts moved these between exempt,
  dividend, and CG treatment.
- Off-market transfers, gifts received then sold: cost and holding period inherit from the previous
  owner (for gifts from relatives).
- Property: stamp-duty value substitutes for consideration when higher (within tolerance);
  54/54EC/54F reinvestment exemptions have lock-ins and deadlines — CA territory if contested.

## Order of set-off inside CG

STCL absorbs STCG then LTCG; LTCL absorbs LTCG only; VDA losses absorb nothing. Apply the 112A
exempt slice AFTER netting. Quarter-wise gain dates feed the advance-tax relief in the interest
computation — keep them.

## Reporting even when zero tax

- LTCG inside the exempt slice: still reported (Schedule 112A + EI).
- Loss years: report to preserve the carry-forward — an unreported loss is a dead loss.
