# Salary head (Schedule S)

## Build the head from evidence

1. One Schedule S row per TAN found in 26AS — including trailing settlements with no Form 16.
2. Gross = 17(1) salary + 17(2) perquisites (from 12BA) + 17(3) profits in lieu. RSU/ESOP vesting
   value sits in 17(2); its later sale is capital gains, not salary.
3. Exemptions under section 10 (old regime; only a few survive the new one — check the registry):
   - **HRA**: least of (actual HRA, rent − 10% of basic, 50%/40% of basic by metro status). Needs
     rent evidence; landlord PAN above the rule threshold.
   - **Leave encashment on exit**: least-of-four statutory formula with a lifetime cap — never the
     full amount received just because it was received.
   - **Gratuity, commuted pension**: statutory formulas, verify caps in the registry.
4. **One standard deduction total** across all employers — the single most common multi-employer
   error, because each employer applied its own.
5. Professional tax actually deducted (16(iii)) — old regime only.

## Multi-employer mechanics

Each employer applied full slabs and possibly different regimes. The return rebuilds the year as
one aggregate under one regime — expect a shortfall whenever there was a job change, and say so
early. The employer's regime flag on Form 16 never constrains the return's regime choice.

## Arrears

Salary arrears received this year for earlier years → relief under section 89 via Form 10E
(file it online BEFORE the return, or the relief is denied in processing).
