# House property and home loans (Schedule HP)

## Establish three facts per property (gap-interview material)

1. **Occupancy**: self-occupied / vacant family use (treated as self-occupied, up to two) /
   let out / under construction (no deduction until possession; pre-construction interest then
   claims in five equal slices).
2. **Ownership shares**: the deed governs, not the loan. A co-borrower who is not an owner gets no
   deduction no matter who pays the EMI. Co-owners split income and interest by share.
3. **Who services the loan**: deduction needs both ownership AND payment. Paying 100% of the EMI on
   a one-third share is a classic over-claim setup — claim conservatively by share unless the
   funding trail clearly supports more.

## Computation

- Self-occupied: annual value nil. Loan interest deductible under the OLD regime only, capped
  (registry). Under the new regime a self-occupied property contributes exactly zero — report the
  property, claim nothing.
- Let out: rent received − municipal taxes paid → NAV; minus 30% standard deduction; minus full
  loan interest. A resulting loss offsets other heads only in the old regime and only up to the cap;
  the rest carries forward inside the HP head. New regime: no inter-head set-off of HP loss at all.
- Principal repayment (and stamp duty in purchase year): old-regime 80C material only.
- Tenant's 194-IB TDS: claim the credit; it also proves the rent figure.

## Interest certificate fine print

Lender certificates split EMI into principal and interest and list prepayments separately — count
prepayment interest in the interest figure and prepaid principal in 80C. Rate resets mid-year mean
the certificate, not EMI × 12, is the truth.
