# Other sources and deductions (Schedule OS / VI-A / EI)

## Other-sources inventory (always run)

| Line | Handling |
|---|---|
| Savings interest | Slab. Per-account from AIS; certificates cross-check |
| FD/RD interest | Slab, on accrual. **Zero TDS proves nothing**: the deduction threshold applies per bank, so interest spread across banks routinely exceeds it in aggregate with no TDS anywhere — the top cause of surprise balance-payable. Total across AIS, never infer from TDS |
| Refund interest | Taxable. Probe: prior-year refund claimed vs credited — the excess is interest even when no statement itemizes it |
| Dividends | Slab; capture quarter-wise dates (ex-date) — they earn advance-tax interest relief |
| Family pension | Slab minus its own standard deduction (registry; differs by regime) |
| Gifts from non-relatives above the threshold | Fully taxable; relatives/wedding/inheritance exempt |
| Lottery, gaming | Flat special rate, no deductions, no set-off |
| PPF interest, exempt dividends | Not taxable but REPORTED in Schedule EI — exempt ≠ omitted |

## Chapter VI-A by regime (claim only what the regime allows)

Old regime: 80C basket, NPS top-up, health insurance, education-loan interest, donations
(registered donees only, cash capped, match against the donee's filed statement), savings-interest
deduction (savings accounts only — never FD/RD; the senior-citizen variant covers both), disability,
rent-without-HRA. Caps in the registry.

New regime survivors: employer NPS (80CCD(2)), Agniveer corpus. That's effectively the list —
when asked "how do I save tax" for a closed year under the new regime, the honest answer is
"you mostly can't; the levers are prospective". Don't invent savings.

Every claimed deduction needs a proof document in the folder or a TODO flag in the data pack.

## Clubbing quick-checks

Minor child's income clubs to the higher-earning parent (small per-child exemption); assets gifted
to a spouse club their income back to the giver. Ask only when a document hints (joint accounts,
minor's PAN in AIS).

## Senior-citizen switches (from DOB, never asked)

60+: higher old-regime exemption, bigger interest deduction, higher health caps; no advance tax if
no business income. 75+ with only pension + interest at one specified bank: filing exemption via
the bank's declaration — verify current conditions before promising it.
