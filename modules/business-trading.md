# Business, profession, and market trading (ITR-3/4, Schedule BP)

## Classify before computing

| Activity | Head | Pool |
|---|---|---|
| Derivatives (index/stock/currency/commodity) | Business | Non-speculative |
| Same-day equity round trips (intraday) | Business | Speculative — a sealed pool: its loss meets only speculative profit |
| Delivery-based buying and selling | Capital gains (business only at genuine dealer scale) |
| Freelance / consulting / professional fees | Profession — 44ADA presumptive usually available |
| Commission / agency | Business, but presumptive 44AD is barred |

One intraday or derivative trade — profit or loss, any size — puts the filer in ITR-3.

## Turnover and the audit gate

- Trading turnover = the absolute-sum convention (|profit| + |loss| per trade). Brokers print it;
  use their figure. Contract value is irrelevant and terrifies people needlessly.
- Audit thresholds live in the registry. Exchange-settled trading is digital by nature, so the
  enhanced threshold almost always applies — most retail traders are orders of magnitude below it.
- **The presumptive trap**: a filer who used presumptive in a recent year and now declares below
  the presumptive rate (or a loss) while income exceeds the basic exemption needs an audit
  regardless of turnover. Check the prior returns before assuming no audit.
- Audit needed → CA, audit-report deadline, later due date. Hand off explicitly.

## Presumptive routes (when they fit)

- 44AD (business) and 44ADA (specified professions): declare the deemed percentage of
  turnover/receipts (registry has limits and rates); no books, no expense claims, **no losses**.
  A loss-making trader must use normal provisions instead.
- 44AD once chosen binds for five years; early exit locks the scheme away and can force audits.
- ITR-4 filers must fill the no-books financial particulars (cash, debtors, creditors, stock) and
  reconcile turnover with GST if registered.

## Normal-provision expenses (trading)

Direct: brokerage, exchange charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty, GST on charges, and — unlike capital
gains — STT. Account-level: platform/auto-square-off charges allocable to trading. Indirect
(internet share, data feeds, advisory): only real, documented amounts; refuse invented round
numbers. Demat AMC belongs to investment holding, not the trading business.

Small-turnover traders: broker statements are the books; use the no-account-case fields plus the
trading-account rows for speculative turnover. Pick the nature-of-business code from the current
utility's dropdown at filing time — codes drift year to year.
